About

I'm a Full Stack Web & Mobile Developer with a passion for building clean, functional, and user-centric digital products. I do my best work at the intersection of thoughtful UI design and solid, maintainable code — where great experience meets scalable architecture.

Currently completing my BS in Information Technology (Digital Arts & Design) at the Technological Institute of the Philippines, I've had the opportunity to build real-world apps during my OJT at NTEK Systems Inc. — from library management systems to AI-powered chatbots and marketplace platforms.

Previously, I developed a full e-commerce platform for Traevents.Co, handling everything from destination browsing and package listings to an admin panel managing content, accounts, and bookings.

Outside of coding, I enjoy exploring design trends, tinkering with mobile apps, and finding creative ways to solve everyday problems with technology. I'm always excited to take on new challenges and grow as a developer.
